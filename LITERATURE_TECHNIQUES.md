# New analysis techniques from the literature corpus (2026-10-27)

**Sources searched:** `~/research-toolkit/md_corpus`, about 17.6k converted documents (Zotero, Drive and Dropbox). A header scan found 388 documents that look like academic papers, and about 30 of them are on equity or quant topics. I also checked `literature_scout.py` (21 covered themes, no open frontier) and `RESEARCH_PAPER.md`.

**Baseline:** the methods `signal_research.py` already uses are rank IC decay with HAC t-statistics, Benjamini-Hochberg FDR, liquidity and volatility neutralisation, bull/bear regime splits, Fama-MacBeth regressions, quintile spreads net of cost with a Deflated Sharpe ratio, a purged walk-forward combination (IC-weighted and ridge), and a trailing-52-week IC health check.

Each technique below is absent from the code. They are ordered by value per unit of effort, given the data the warehouse already holds.

| 1 | **Overnight vs intraday return decomposition**: split each signal's quintile spread into close→open and open→close legs | Lou, Polk & Skouras, *A Tug of War: Overnight Versus Intraday Expected Returns* (`XL_3a2718d9ba09_445bce5….md`) | The paper finds momentum and reversal profits earned entirely overnight, with the intraday leg often of the opposite sign. The smoothed overnight-minus-intraday spread also predicts the strategy's future performance, which would give a timing signal for the `ic_health` monitor. | Needs **Open**: the loader currently pivots only Close/High/Volume (`signal_research.py:~98`). | 2 | **Probability of Backtest Overfitting (CSCV) + combinatorial purged CV** | Palomar, *Backtesting Portfolios* (HKUST MAFS5310), sections on multiple randomized backtesting and combinatorial purged CV (`XL_088a0ed501a1_slides_backtesting.md`). Also Olorunnimbe & Viktor 2022, *Deep learning in the stock market: a systematic survey of practice, backtesting…* (`XL_cec33b90172c_s10462-022-10226-0.md`) | The Deflated Sharpe ratio is computed for each strategy, and the walk-forward runs a single path. PBO estimates how often the in-sample best of the ~165 variants lands below the median out of sample, which is a direct number for the "no DSR > 0.95" verdict. CPCV gives a distribution of out-of-sample Sharpe ratios rather than one figure. | Existing return matrix (variants × weeks) | Low |
| 3 | **Learning-to-rank combination** (pairwise/listwise: LambdaMART, ListNet) | Poh, Lim, Zohren & Roberts, *Building Cross-Sectional Systematic Strategies by Learning to Rank* (`XL_5be20d2b561d_webfetch-1788318686388-ae3m9u.md`) | Study 6 combines signals with IC weights or ridge, both pointwise. The paper reports about 3× the Sharpe ratio of regress-then-sort on momentum. It slots into `purged_walk_forward` as a third model alongside ridge, with the same purging and embargo. | Existing 11 signals | Medium (needs `lightgbm`, which is not installed) |
| 4 | **Price-band / circuit "magnet effect" tradeability filter** | Chen, Petukhov & Wang, *The Dark Side of Circuit Breakers* (`XL_595953729c74_ZVDWPZQU_Circuitbreaker.md`). Also *Market-wide circuit breakers: impact on stock-market stability in India* (`XL_07b87cb9b3e5_….md`) | India's per-stock bands (2/5/10/20%) can lock a stock at its limit, so a hi52 or momentum "buy" at the upper limit may not be fillable, and that inflates the edge. Two tests: (a) drop formation days on which the close equals the band limit and rerun India hi52/mom12_1; (b) test closeness to the band as a signal in its own right (the magnet effect). | Bhavcopy carries band and price; otherwise infer from \|ret\| ≈ band | Low–medium |
| 5 | **Kalman-filter reversal signal + time-varying IC** | Liu & Zhu 2024, *Analysis of market efficiency in main stock markets using the Kalman filter* (arXiv 2404.16449; `XL_100f1cd28b02_2404.16449v1.md`). Cited in `RESEARCH_PAPER.md` but not implemented. | The paper reports reversal returns from the filtered-trend deviation in **Korea**, Vietnam and Malaysia, and positive returns in developed markets. That fits the re-test's Korea-only reversal. Two uses: (a) a new `kf_rev` signal family; (b) replace the fixed 52-week window in `ic_health` with a local-level Kalman filter on the weekly IC, which adapts faster to signal decay. | Close only | Low |
| 6 | **Covariance filtering for the long-short book** (Average Oracle vs DCC + non-linear shrinkage) | Bongiorno, Challet et al., *Covariance matrix filtering and portfolio optimisation: the Average Oracle vs non-linear shrinkage* (`XL_02c89967a55e_webfetch-1789179495447-6ldj6j.md`) | The study's books are equal-weight quintiles. Risk-weighting them with a filtered covariance matrix should lift the Sharpe ratio at a fixed signal. The paper finds the Average Oracle beats DCC+NLS after turnover, which matters here because the 73% turnover is what kills RSI-oversold. | Returns panel | Medium |
| 7 | **Identification through heteroskedasticity for cross-market lead-lag** | Weber, *Correlation vs. Causality in Stock Market Comovement* (SFB 649 DP 2007-064; `XL_ace983c3dd1f_64.md`) | `peer_network.py` found industry reversal (IC −0.08) using plain correlation. This method separates direct spillover from a common factor, e.g. whether Korean small caps lead Korean large caps, or US overnight leads India/Korea. That makes it the structural version of the lead-lag idea. | Index or size-bucket returns | Medium–high |
| 8 | **Tail-aware risk measures in construction/evaluation** (CVaR, deviation measures) | Ortobelli, Rachev, Stoyanov, Fabozzi, *The Proper Use of Risk Measures in Portfolio Theory* (`XL_709692bf2cc3_ZVJRGX8Y_RiskMeasures_IJRAF.md`) | Every verdict now rests on Sharpe or DSR. Short-leg strategies (hi52 bottom quintile) carry crash risk, and the right risk measure differs by market. Reporting CVaR 95%, the worst month and the drawdown next to each DSR would show this. | Existing returns | Low |

## Status
Techniques 1, 2 and 8 were implemented on 2026-10-27 in `signal_validation.py` (`overnight_intraday_fwd`, `pbo_cscv`, `tail_risk`, all unit-tested) and `signal_research.py`. Results are in sections 5, 5b and 5c of [SIGNAL_RESEARCH_RESULTS.md](SIGNAL_RESEARCH_RESULTS.md), and findings 7–9 are in [SIGNAL_RESEARCH.md](SIGNAL_RESEARCH.md). Lou, Polk & Skouras's timing signal (the smoothed overnight-minus-intraday spread) is not built yet.

## Suggested order
1. Run **1 + 2 + 8** in one pass. They are cheap, need no new libraries, and directly stress-test the current verdicts: does India hi52 survive when split overnight/intraday, and what is PBO across the 165 variants?
2. Then run **4**, an India-specific check that could cut or confirm the one surviving long-short edge.
3. Then **5** (adds a signal and improves health monitoring) and **3** (a better combiner).
4. Then **6** and **7**, which are portfolio-construction and structural research.

## Caveats
- The corpus is mostly non-finance. The ~30 equity papers are what it holds, not a full survey. To widen the search, run `literature_scout.py --query …` online against OpenAlex/arXiv, and add the new themes (overnight/intraday, learning to rank, PBO, price limits) to `FRONTIER_THEMES`.
- The claims above come from abstracts and slides read in this pass, not from replicating the papers' numbers.
